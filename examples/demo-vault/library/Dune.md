# Dune

#book

An epic science fiction novel set on the desert planet Arrakis. The story follows Paul Atreides as he becomes entangled in political intrigue, religious prophecy, and the struggle for control of the valuable spice melange. The book explores themes of power, ecology, and human potential across multiple generations. I am still reading this masterpiece. See also [[Notes on sci-fi]] for related genre analysis.
